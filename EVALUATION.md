# Evaluation plan

**Proposed checks — not completed test results.**

## Generator checks

Record the full prompt, input values, model/version, date and generated output. Use varied subjects, grades, durations and learner needs.

| Check | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Completeness | All six requested sections are present and usable. |
| Alignment | Each objective is connected to an activity and an assessment that can measure it. |
| Timing | Activity, setup and transition times fit the total duration. |
| Differentiation | Accommodations are specific and practical, rather than generic labels. |
| Theory | The rationale connects the selected teaching theory with actual design choices. |
| Cognitive load | Risks identify concrete overload points and mitigation options. |
| Tool feasibility | Suggested digital tools exist and fit the classroom's access and setup constraints. |

## Critique extension checks

Before describing Critique Mode as implemented, retain a screenshot or recording of the separate review interface and a complete generation-to-review run.

Compare review of a reasonable plan against a deliberately flawed plan, including mismatched assessment, excessive timing, inaccessible activities and missing student choice. Record whether the critique detects each seeded issue and whether suggested changes address it.

A teacher should independently assess the plans and compare that assessment with the AI review. A high AI score alone is not evidence of plan quality. Repeated runs are needed before reporting consistency or error rates.

## Reporting

Keep intended behavior, observed behavior and proposed improvements separate. Do not report teacher adoption, preparation-time reduction or quality improvement without actual records and a defined measurement method.
